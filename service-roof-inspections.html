Free Roof Inspections

Comprehensive roof assessment with honest recommendations. No pressure, no obligation — just expert advice from certified inspectors.

Your roof protects everything you value — but most homeowners have no idea what condition it is in until a leak appears. Amstill Roofing offers free, no-obligation roof inspections for homeowners across Houston and Round Rock. Our certified inspectors assess every component of your roofing system and provide a clear, honest report on its condition, expected remaining life, and any issues that need attention.

During the inspection, we examine shingles, flashing, pipe boots, ridge vents, soffit, fascia, gutters, and the visible roof deck. We check for storm damage, wear patterns, improper previous repairs, and ventilation issues. The entire process takes about 30 to 45 minutes, and you receive a written report with photos documenting our findings — whether the news is good or bad.

We believe in honesty over sales. If your roof has 10 or more years of life remaining, we will tell you. If minor repairs can extend its lifespan, we will recommend those over a full replacement. Our inspectors are trained to give you the truth, not a sales pitch. That integrity is why we have earned over 1,400 five-star reviews and an A-plus BBB rating over 50 years in business.

We recommend scheduling a roof inspection after any major storm, if you notice granules in your gutters, if your roof is over 15 years old, or before buying or selling a home. Amstill also provides pre-listing inspection reports for real estate transactions. Call us or book online — our team will be at your door within days, and often within 24 hours.

FAQ

Roof Inspection Questions

Is the roof inspection really free with no obligation?
+

Absolutely. Our roof inspections are 100% free with zero obligation. We'll provide a detailed report of your roof's condition, identify any issues, and give you honest recommendations — whether that means a simple repair, continued monitoring, or eventual replacement.

How often should I have my roof inspected?
+

We recommend a professional roof inspection at least once a year and after any major storm. Regular inspections catch small problems before they become expensive repairs. For roofs over 15 years old, semi-annual inspections are ideal to extend your roof's lifespan.

What does a roof inspection include?
+

Our comprehensive inspection covers shingle condition, flashing integrity, gutter and drainage systems, ventilation, soffit and fascia, chimney and skylight seals, signs of water intrusion, attic ventilation, and structural integrity. You'll receive a detailed written report with photos.

How long does a typical roof inspection take?
+

A thorough roof inspection usually takes 45 minutes to 1 hour depending on the size and complexity of your roof. We take our time to ensure nothing is missed, including a careful examination of both the exterior and accessible interior/attic areas.

Can a roof inspection help if I'm buying or selling a home?
+

Yes, a roof inspection is highly valuable in real estate transactions. For buyers, it reveals the true condition and remaining life of the roof. For sellers, a clean inspection report adds confidence and value. We provide detailed, professional reports suitable for real estate negotiations.
